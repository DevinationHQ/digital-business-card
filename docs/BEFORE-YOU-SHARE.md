# Before you share your Digital Business Card

Open your published card on your phone and work through this list. Copying or downloading JSON and previewing a draft do not update the published website.

- [ ] Confirm your complete card JSON was saved in `data/card.json` and the deployment succeeded; copying alone does not update the site.
- [ ] Open your main published address without `?example=` or `?preview=`. Check your name, role, introduction and every project link.
- [ ] Replace the example contacts and identity media. Leave contact fields blank if you do not want them public. Check Call, Email and Message point to your intended details.
- [ ] Check your portrait loads. If you use a video, test Play/Pause and the photo fallback. Keep the original media somewhere safe; publish a compressed delivery copy you have permission to use.
- [ ] Download **Save contact** and open the `.vcf` on your phone. Check the name, contact details and website before saving.
- [ ] Open **Share card** and check the full published address includes your repository path. Clear or correct the editor's published-link field if it points somewhere else.
- [ ] Download a fresh QR from the published card and scan it with another device. Confirm it opens your card. Never print a localhost QR. Make a new QR whenever the published address changes.
- [ ] Check at phone width and with your device's reduced-motion setting. Confirm text and buttons are usable, video stays still until requested with reduced motion, and there is no sideways page scroll.
- [ ] Check the public repository and media contain only information you intend to publish. A public card and its files can be read by anyone. Keep licence and dependency notices.
- [ ] Save your downloaded `card.json` and source copy so you can edit again. Community membership is optional.

Something missing? Follow [SETUP.md](SETUP.md), [AI-SETUP.md](AI-SETUP.md) or [VIDEO-GUIDE.md](VIDEO-GUIDE.md). Use the template repository's [Issues](https://github.com/DevinationHQ/digital-business-card/issues) for a reproducible problem, with synthetic details and no private contact information.
